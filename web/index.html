What am I playing?

The jet is flown by the v3 build of a research project: a dynamic scene graph of everything in the air, a temporal graph neural network (117k parameters) that infers each missile’s hidden guidance from radar tracks alone, and a graph world model that rolls 58 candidate manoeuvres 72 frames into the future every frame and flies the cheapest one.

Your missiles fly in four styles — straight pursuit, flanking, weaving and erratic darts — and seeker missiles steer around terrain. The jet starts each life with no flares and earns one volley per cluster you launch, so dumping clusters arms its countermeasures.

The jet shoots back every 4 seconds. Lose every battery and you lose.
